Android app

Compare straps on one timeline.

MultiPulse scans for standard BLE Heart Rate devices, shows live bpm from each strap, and records a session you can review, export, or share.

  • Multi-strap capture Connect up to four monitors and keep every sample on the same clock.
  • Agreement review After recording, see nearest-peer agreement and a time-versus-bpm chart.
  • Broadcast one bpm Advertise a single, average, or Wizard reading to a bike computer or another phone.

How it works

From straps to a shared signal.

  1. 01

    Scan & connect

    Wake your straps, pick up to four Heart Rate devices, and wait for live bpm.

  2. 02

    Record & review

    Start a session, then open the report to see agreement scores and the comparison chart.

  3. 03

    Broadcast or export

    Share one bpm over Bluetooth, or export the session JSON for later analysis.
